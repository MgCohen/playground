M1 · G1

Swipe release

The card must leave in the direction of the act. A short drag goes back with a small spring.

AFly outThe card keeps the drag speed, turns 18° and fades in 300 ms. A button act throws it the same way.
BPressThe stamp stays. The card presses flat and fades in place in 260 ms.

Build: swipe.js adds a class and clicks the action button on animationend. About 15 lines more.

M2 · G2

Skip to the back

Press the down button or drag down. The user must see that the card is still in the pile.

ATuck underThe card drops 180 ms, goes behind the pile, then rises into the last slot. The next card lifts to the top.
BSinkThe card drops and fades in 240 ms. It shows again at the back.

Build: two classes and one transitionend in swipe.js. Blazor moves the card in the order after the animation.

M3 · G3

Card enter and leave

The pile shows up to three real cards. Each card behind is 8 px lower and 4.5 % smaller.

AReal pileWhen the top card leaves, each card moves up one slot in 320 ms. A new first card drops in from the top with a small spring.
BFadeThe cards change with a 200 ms fade. No position motion.

Build: Cards.razor renders the top three cards with @key and --i. CSS only.

M4 · G4

View change

Tap the app bar. The header and the app bar stay. Only the page body moves. The nav dot moves to the new item.

ARiseThe new page fades in and rises 10 px in 240 ms. Calm, and the same for each view.
BSlideThe page slides 28 px from the side of the tapped item. It shows the place in the app bar.
CRise and staggerRise, but each block starts 40 ms after the block above it. More motion.

Build: a @key on the page in MainLayout and one CSS animation. B needs the old and new index (small C#).

M5 · G5

Sheets and toast

The answer sheet and the filter sheet slide up over a fading scrim and slide down when they close. The undo toast drops from the top. A bar under it counts down the 5 s undo time.

AMotionSheet up in 340 ms, down in 220 ms. Toast drop with a small spring. Undo bar 5 s, linear.

Build: CSS for enter. The close waits for animationend (small JavaScript), then Blazor removes the sheet.

M6 · G6

Loading states

The sample loads for 1.6 s, then shows the Home view. Press Replay to see it again.

ASkeletonRaised blocks in the place of the real cards, with carved lines and a slow shine. The content then fades in.
BHome pulseThe orange home circle in the center breathes until the app is ready.

Build: static HTML in index.html and App.razor before Blazor starts. Blazor replaces it. CSS only.
