The text is a quest. Each need is one of these kinds:

- `tag`: a skill that the party must have. The name is one of the known tags. No amount.
- `stat`: a minimum of a stat for the whole party. The name is one of the known stats. The amount is the minimum.
- `budget`: the most gold that the party can cost. The name is `budget`. The amount is the gold.

Give at most one budget. If the text gives no amount of gold, give no budget need. Leave `missing` empty: a quest with no budget has no budget.
